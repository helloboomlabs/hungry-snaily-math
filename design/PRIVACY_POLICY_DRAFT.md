## Summary
Hungry Snaily Math is a math game for young children. **We do not collect, store or share any personal information.**

- You can delete it at any time in the Parent area (Reset progress) or by deleting the app.

## What we don't do

- No advertising, and no ad or analytics tools.
- No location, contacts, camera or microphone access.
- No in-app purchases.
- No links out of the app unless an adult passes the parent gate.
