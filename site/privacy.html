Hungry Snaily Math – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026
Developer: BoomLabs · Contact: helloboomlabs@gmail.com

Summary

Hungry Snaily Math is a math game for young children. We do not collect, store or share any personal information.

What the app stores

  • Game progress (levels, medals, items, settings and play time) is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • You can delete it at any time in the Parent area (Reset progress) or by deleting the app.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-in, names or photos.
  • No advertising, and no ad or analytics tools.
  • No location, contacts, camera or microphone access.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • No links out of the app.

Internet use

Snaily's voice. When the device is online, the sentence Snaily is about to say (only the game's own story text, never anything the child types) is sent to Google's text-to-speech service to create her kid voice. Google therefore receives that text and the device's IP address. Grown-ups can turn Snaily's voice off in the Parent area to stop this. When offline, the device's built-in voice is used and nothing is sent.

The free web version is loaded from GitHub Pages and keeps working offline after it loads. GitHub, as the host, may keep standard server logs (such as IP address) when the page is opened; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

The app is designed for children aged 6–8 and follows the Apple App Store Kids Category rules. Because we collect no personal information, there is no data for us to access, change or delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the policy in the app.
