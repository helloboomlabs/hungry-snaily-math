Hungry Snaily Math – Support

A math game for ages 6–8 on iPhone and iPad, by BoomLabs. Free, offline, no ads, no in-app purchases, no data collected.

Contact

Email helloboomlabs@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 working days.

Parent area

On the home screen, press and hold the gear button for 2 seconds, then answer the question. There you can turn sound, voice and haptics on or off, set a break reminder, see progress, and reset progress.

No voice?

Snaily uses the speech voices built into iOS. Check that the ring/silent switch is on ring and the volume is up. For a nicer voice, download an enhanced English voice in Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices.
