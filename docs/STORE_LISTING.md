## Description
Snaily the snail is hungry, and only maths can feed her!

Hungry Snaily Math is a gentle, playful maths game for children aged 6 to 8 (US Grades 1–3). It has two gardens to explore:

NUMBER GARDEN: Build 3-digit numbers with place-value blocks, then add and take away, trading tens and hundreds along the way. Each right answer earns leaves for Snaily's tummy.

STORY WORLD: Walk a garden map with 9 places to visit. Each one teaches a topic through short word problems: adding and taking away, comparing, two-step stories, mental maths, multiplying and sharing, money, measuring, time, and fractions.

Children learn the methods teachers use: bar models, number bonds, comparison bars, guess and check, make a ten, near doubles, arrays and more. Snaily shows each method first, then lets your child try.
